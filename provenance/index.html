Retrieval note

The temple's own site disallows automated reading at its root. Facts above were taken from the temple's individually published pages and its own hosted PDF documents, reached through search rather than by reading the site wholesale. No page was fetched more than once, and nothing here was generated from a model's recollection of the temple.

How to correct this page

  1. Open an issue on the repository issue tracker, or send the correction to the steward.
  2. Corrections are appended and dated. Nothing is silently overwritten.
  3. A confirmed fact moves from Needs confirmation to Confirmed by community with the date and the person who confirmed it.
