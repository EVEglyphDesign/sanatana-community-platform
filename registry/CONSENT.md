# Consent register

No named person, photograph, age, school or donation figure enters this repository until a
consent row exists here. Consent names the layer and the reader. Withdrawal is one request and
takes effect in the next commit.

| Date (UTC) | Subject | Given by | Layers consented | Readers | Withdrawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | — | — | — | — |

## Layers

- `institution` — address, hours, governance, contact routes
- `calendar` — festivals and recurring observances
- `education` — ladder progress, recorded per cohort by default
- `service` — seva entries, aggregate by default
- `named` — any personally identifying detail, including photographs

## Readers

- `public` — the published GitHub Pages surface
- `community` — repository collaborators only
- `steward` — the steward alone

Default for every subject, absent a row above: `education` and `service` at cohort/aggregate
level only, nothing under `named`, reader `public`.
